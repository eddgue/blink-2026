Put every pin on your Google Map
  1. Download blink-2026-pins.csv
  2. On a laptop, open google.com/mymaps and create a new map.
  3. Click Import and upload the CSV. For position, pick the Address column. For the title, pick Name.
  4. Under the layer, set Style by Zone or Type to color the pins.
  5. On your phone, open Google Maps, go to You (or Saved), then Maps. The BLINK map shows over your live location.

Pins use the nearest intersection or venue from the official BLINK zone maps, so expect to look around once you arrive. Schedule from blinkcincinnati.com as of Oct 8. Times are Eastern.
